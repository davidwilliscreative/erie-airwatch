# Methodology changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-22)
Initial definitions: zone overflights (segment intersection, 120 s gap limit, 120 s re-entry cooldown),
candidate circuits (runway pass to runway pass, downwind then final), likely touch-and-go (approach + departure,
≤100 ft above field, ≥35 kt, ≤90 s), probable full stop, low approach. Data-quality score from density,
continuity, altitude consistency, runway proximity and feed coverage.
